About Me

I'm Kelsey Howell-Conde, an operations and project management professional who builds order out of complexity. Over 6+ years in high-volume, multi-stakeholder environments, I've run the full lifecycle of concurrent, multi-month projects, coordinating budgets, timelines, and cross-functional teams from kickoff to close.

My background is in residential homebuilding, where I managed 20+ concurrent client projects at a time, each with its own budget, timeline, and network of dependencies spanning buyers, agents, lenders, title companies, and construction teams. I coordinated nearly $50M in transactions this way, and I did it well enough to earn three consecutive GHBA Million Dollar Circle Awards for performance and reliability.

But my real specialty is what happens behind the scenes: identifying process gaps, designing the fix, and making it repeatable. I built a Salesforce implementation playbook proposing a 3-phase roadmap to consolidate 6+ disconnected sales systems into one unified workflow. I analyzed 750 tasks across 30 cross-functional projects to build an operations dashboard surfacing schedule delays, budget performance, and portfolio risk. And when outreach processes were stuck at a few hundred contacts a week, I designed an AI-assisted workflow that scaled it into the thousands, without losing accuracy or personalization.

I use AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT daily as a working practice, not a novelty, for process documentation, workflow design, and problem-solving. I'm HubSpot Inbound and Service Hub Certified and actively working toward my Salesforce Administrator Certification.

If you're here, thanks for taking a look.
